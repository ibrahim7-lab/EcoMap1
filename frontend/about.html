ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology for a cleaner community.

EcoMap brings environmental issue reporting and location mapping into one simple place.

🌍

Why EcoMap?

Local environmental problems can be hard to document and follow up on. EcoMap helps people share clear reports and gives the community a way to see where issues are happening.

🧭

Location matters

Reports can include coordinates and appear on an interactive map, making it easier to understand where attention may be needed.

🤝

Progress together

Reports have statuses so visitors can follow public progress while administrators review and update submissions.

Built with understandable tools

HTML creates the pages, CSS styles them, and JavaScript handles forms and API requests. Node.js and Express provide the backend, MySQL stores data, and Leaflet powers the map.
